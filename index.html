How to play

Eat crystal apples to grow and score. Don't hit the walls, the pillars or your own body. Every camera plays the same game, so switch any time.

Overhead view

  • ↑↓←→ or WASD move in that screen direction
  • Swipe (touch) move in the swipe direction

Chase and first-person views

  • ←→ or AD turn left / right
  • Tap or swipe left / right (touch) turn
  • Arrows on the floor show your path; the compass and minimap point to food

Everywhere

  • QE turn left / right relative to the snake
  • Space hold Rush: faster, double points, uses the meter
  • C cycle camera: overhead, chase, first-person
  • P / Esc pause · R restart · M mute
  • Gamepad D-pad / stick to steer, A rush, Y camera, Start pause

Pickups

  • Crystal apple 10 points, grows 1, adds Rush.
  • Prism 50 points, grows 2, fills Rush. Gone in 9 s.
  • Orb chain numbered orbs: eat them in order for rising points, a big bonus and a full Rush meter. Eat one out of order and the chain breaks. Chain orbs don't make you longer.
  • Golden mouse runs away from you. Corner it for 100 points.
  • Boost pad a free burst of speed and double points.

Speed levels and zones

  • Eat again within 5 s to build a combo, up to x8.
  • Every 4 apples the speed level rises. Some level-ups raise pillars: a red ring on the floor warns you where.
  • Every 3 levels the world transforms into a new zone with its own music.
