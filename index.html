How to play

Eat crystal apples to grow and score. Don't hit the walls, the pillars or your own body. Every camera plays the same game, so switch any time.

Overhead view

  • ↑↓←→ or WASD move in that screen direction
  • Swipe (touch) move in the swipe direction

Chase and first-person views

  • ←→ or AD turn left / right
  • Tap or swipe left / right (touch) turn
  • Arrows on the floor show your path; the compass and minimap point to food
  • Sunset Heavens (zone 5) jetpack flight: ↑ climb, ↓ dive (the other key levels out), ← → turn; on touch, swipe or use ▲ ▼

Everywhere

  • QE turn left / right relative to the snake
  • Space hold Rush: faster, double points, uses the meter
  • C cycle camera: chase, first-person, overhead
  • G ghost guide: race a perfect bot
  • T speedrun timer on / off
  • P / Esc pause · R restart · M next song · N mute
  • Gamepad D-pad / stick to steer, A rush, Y camera, Start pause

Pickups

  • Crystal apple 10 points, grows 1, adds Rush.
  • Prism 50 points, grows 2, fills Rush. Gone in 9 s.
  • Orb chain numbered orbs: eat them in order for rising points, a big bonus and a full Rush meter. Eat one out of order and the chain breaks. Chain orbs don't make you longer.
  • Golden mouse runs away from you. Corner it for 100 points.
  • Boost pad a free burst of speed and double points.

Speed levels and zones

  • Eat again within 5 s to build a combo, up to x8.
  • Eating apples raises your speed level: 3 apples per level in the first zone, more later. From the second zone, some level-ups raise pillars: a red ring on the floor warns you where.
  • Every 3 levels (2 in Sunset Heavens) the world transforms into a new zone with its own music.

Beat the game

  • Clear Sunset Heavens and the snake jumps to hyperspeed, dropping out onto a board floating over a black hole.
  • The black hole is eating the board: every apple dooms a few cells, spiralling in from the edge. They glow, then fall in once you're off them. Don't slide off the edge!
  • Fill every cell that's left to finish the galaxy and win the game: 100%. The board shrinks to meet you, and it never cuts you off from your own tail.

Bonus level

  • Grow to length 30 in the Galactic Core to unlock it. Classic Snake on a 17 x 17 board: no power-ups, no speed-ups, every golden apple grows you by one, and Rush still works.
  • It starts in complete darkness, with only the golden apple lit. Eat ten and the party starts.
